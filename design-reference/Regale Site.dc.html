Backwaters · Alleppey, Kerala

The Alleppey Ledger

A restored spice merchant's home on the banks of Lake Vembanad, where the ledgers of a 19th-century cardamom trade still line the study walls. Nine rooms, one long verandah, and a kitchen that cooks whatever the morning boat brings in.

Mornings begin with mist on the water and end with toddy-shop fish curry on the pier. The house is deliberately unhurried: no televisions, no piped music, no check-in queue — your host meets you at the jetty.
